Theme Notes

A few guardrails for extending the theme

theme notes hugo

The fastest way to extend this setup is to keep the vendored theme unchanged and add site-specific files only where needed.

Boundaries

  • Theme files stay under themes/loficode-hugo-theme/
  • Content stays under content/
  • Shared images stay under static/site-images/
