This starter keeps the theme files under `themes/` and the images you will edit most often under `static/site-images/`.

{{< coffee-break title="Quick Note" >}}
Swap `profile.png` and `hero.png` in one place, then reuse them across pages.
{{< /coffee-break >}}

## Included

- `content/` for pages and posts
- `themes/loficode-hugo-theme/` for the vendored theme
- `static/site-images/` for shared images
